> Crownfall. The old king is dead, his crown shattered into five shards held by rival powers; a blight (the Rot) is spreading. You are an exiled squire turned wandering knight. Original IP only. Medieval fantasy: castles, villages, knights, mages, dragons, the Rot undead.

## The World — Aethelgard

A continent shaped by the fall of the Sunsworn King. His crown — the **Aureate Diadem** — held the land in balance. When it shattered, five shards scattered, and the Rot seeped from the wounds in the world.

**Tone:** Melancholic grandeur. Beauty persists, but decay encroaches. Golden wheat fields rot at the edges. Ancient forests whisper of things older than kings.

**Era:** Late medieval. Plate armor exists but is rare. Most folk wear wool and leather. Magic is feared and regulated by the Mages' Compact.

---

## The Rot

A supernatural blight, not a disease. It rewrites flesh and stone.

| Stage    | Effect                                    |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Seepage  | Crops wilt, water turns brackish          |
| Bloom    | Animals mutate, fog glows faintly green   |
| Reaping  | Dead rise as Hollowed; stone cracks       |
| Maw      | A rift opens; the land dies               |

The Rot accelerates near broken shards. Stopping it requires reuniting the Diadem — or destroying it forever.

## The Five Shards

| Shard       | Holder                  | Region    |
| ----------- | ----------------------- | --------- |
| Dawn        | The Harvest Queen       | Greenmarch |
| Root        | The Thornmother (fey)   | Thornwood |
| Anvil       | Forge-Lord Kaelin       | Ironcrag  |
| Tide        | The Salt Baron          | Saltfen   |
| Ember       | Vaeltharax (dragon)     | Ashfell   |

Each holder believes their shard is the *true* crown. None will yield willingly.

## Regions

### 1 · Greenmarch — The Golden Start
- **Biome:** Rolling farmland, orchards, rivers.
- **Palette:** Gold, green, warm amber. Golden-hour default light.
- **Settlements:** 2 villages (Briarhollow, Millhaven), 1 market town (Goldacre).
- **Threat:** Rot seepage at the eastern border. Bandits.
- **Mood:** Peaceful but haunted. The player's origin.

### 2 · Thornwood — The Whispering Wood
- **Biome:** Ancient forest, giant trees, perpetual mist.
- **Palette:** Deep green, silver, violet.
- **Settlements:** 1 hidden fey village (Elmsong), 1 ranger outpost.
- **Threat:** The Thornmother's domain. Illusions. Rot-corrupted beasts.
- **Mood:** Enchanted, disorienting, beautiful and dangerous.

### 3 · Ironcrag — The Mountain Holds
- **Biome:** Snow-capped peaks, deep mines, frozen waterfalls.
- **Palette:** Steel grey, white, iron-blue.
- **Settlements:** 2 mine-holds (Deepdelve, Frostkeep), 1 fortress (Anvilgate).
- **Threat:** Avalanche, cave-ins, Forge-Lord's iron guard.
- **Mood:** Harsh, stoic, industrial. Dwarven-adjacent but human.

### 4 · Saltfen — The Drowned Coast
- **Biome:** Marsh, tidal flats, crumbling ports, shipwrecks.
- **Palette:** Teal, rust, fog-white.
- **Settlements:** 1 port town (Tidegate), 2 fishing hamlets.
- **Threat:** Pirates, drownings, the Salt Baron's tax collectors.
- **Mood:** Salty, desperate, mercantile. Smuggler's paradise.

### 5 · Ashfell — The Dragon's Wastes
- **Biome:** Volcanic badlands, lava rivers, obsidian spires.
- **Palette:** Crimson, black, ember-orange.
- **Settlements:** 1 ruined temple (Emberheart), 0 safe towns.
- **Threat:** Vaeltharax. Fire elementals. Rot at its most advanced.
- **Mood:** Apocalyptic. The end of the world.

---

## Highspire — The Capital

Sits at the geographic centre where all five roads converge.

- **The Shattered Throne:** The castle where the Diadem broke. Now a ruin at the city's heart, ringed by the new palace.
- **Population:** ~15,000 (largest settlement).
- **Factions:** The Regent's Council (5 seats, one per shard-holder's emissary).
- **Role:** Hub for endgame. Political intrigue. The player's choices reshape the Council.

## Visual Identity

- **Lighting:** Cinematic. Golden hour default. Dramatic shadows.
- **Architecture:** Real medieval — no fantasy chrome. Stone, timber, thatch.
- **Nature:** Exaggerated scale. Trees taller than cathedrals. Mountains that block the sun.
- **The Rot:** Green-black bioluminescence. Organic corruption. Beautiful in a wrong way.
- **UI:** Minimal. Parchment textures. Hand-drawn map elements.

---

## Pillars (World)

1. **Awe at scale** — Every vista should make the player stop.
2. **Living world** — NPCs have schedules. Weather changes. Crops grow.
3. **Showcase visuals** — This is the game you screenshot.
4. **Original IP** — No Tolkien clones. Every name, every lore, is ours.
